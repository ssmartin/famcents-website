Support

We’re happy to help.

Contact

The quickest way: in FamCents, go to Settings → Contact support. When asked, choose “Include” to add diagnostic info: the app version, your phone model and what the app did recently (screens, syncs, errors). It never contains your amounts, notes or names, you see all of it in the email before you send it, and it helps us find and fix the problem much faster. We usually reply within two working days.

Can’t open the app? Email smartin.appsupport@gmail.com and mention your phone model and whether you use iPhone or Android.

Because FamCents keeps your data on your phone, we can’t see your transactions. Never send us backups or screenshots with personal details unless we ask for something specific.

Sharing with your partner

Can I use FamCents on my own?

Yes. Everything works on one phone, and syncing is optional: set it up whenever you like, or turn it off in Settings → Sharing → “Partner sync”.

How do I set up sync with my partner?

You only do this once. Both of you need FamCents installed.

  1. On one phone, tap “Set up” on the Overview’s “Share with your partner” card (or Settings → Sharing) and enter your name. Your partner sees it next to the entries you add.
  2. Tap “Send to partner”. On iPhone, choose your partner’s iPhone in AirDrop. On Android, send it with Quick Share, WhatsApp, Mail or any other app.
  3. Your partner opens the file and FamCents syncs by itself. On Android with Quick Share, tap “View downloads”, then the file (Quick Share’s own “Open” button tries Google Photos, which can’t open it). If Android asks which app to use, choose FamCents → “Just once”.
  4. Your partner taps “Send mine to …” and sends their file back the same way, so you get their entries too.

That’s it: your phones are now paired, and both have the same transactions.

How do we sync after that?

Tap “Sync” on the Overview whenever you like, for example once a day or at the end of the week. The line above it shows when you last synced (“Synced with Ana · Today, 18:30”).

Sync first tries the quick way, without a file:

  • Two Android phones on the same Wi-Fi (beta): turn on Settings → Sharing → “Sync on the same Wi-Fi (beta)” on both phones and have FamCents open on both.
  • Two iPhones close to each other: with “Sync directly when nearby” on.

One tap then updates both phones, with nothing to send or accept. If your partner’s phone isn’t found within a few seconds (FamCents closed, another network, guest Wi-Fi, an iPhone and an Android phone), Sync simply sends a file like the first time.

Does sync work between an iPhone and an Android phone?

Yes, with a file each time: send it with WhatsApp, Mail or any app you both use. The quick sync without a file works between two iPhones or between two Android phones.

What is shared, and what stays on each phone?

Shared: transactions, categories, budgets, accounts and recurring transactions. Each phone keeps its own currency, reminders, app lock, appearance and Pro.

After the first sync, every entry remembers who added it: you see your partner’s name on their entries, “Who spent what” in Statistics, and an “Added by” filter in Search.

What happens if we both changed the same expense?

FamCents merges both phones: new entries from either side are added, deletions carry over, and if the same entry was changed on both phones, the most recent change is kept. If something looks wrong after a sync, open Settings → Sharing → your partner’s name and tap “Undo last sync”.

The file doesn’t open in FamCents
  • “Unsupported file type” (Android, Quick Share): tap “View downloads”, then the file, instead of “Open”.
  • Android asks which app to use: choose FamCents → “Just once”.
  • Nothing happens, or the app can’t read it (some email apps): save the file to your phone, then in FamCents go to Settings → Sharing → “Open a received file” and pick it.
  • “This is your own file”: you opened the file you sent. Open the one your partner sent you.
Sync shows “Partner” instead of my partner’s name

Entries are labelled by the phone that added them. “Partner” means a phone FamCents doesn’t know by name, for example after your partner reinstalled FamCents or used another phone. Ask your partner to set their name (Settings → Sharing) and sync again; entries added from then on carry their name.

Do we both need Pro?

Usually not. Partner sync is free for everyone, and one Pro is enough for most couples: the budgets and recurring transactions the Pro partner sets up sync to both phones, so you both see them. The second phone only needs Pro for its own statistics beyond the month, Excel reports and accounts.

On iPhone, one Pro purchase covers your whole Apple Family Sharing group anyway. On Android, Pro is bought per Google account.

Pro and purchases

How do I get Pro back on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Open the Pro screen (Settings → the Pro card) and tap “Restore purchase”, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

What is the founding member offer?

Everyone who gets FamCents in its first weeks gets Pro free for life. On iPhone, the App Store remembers when you first downloaded the app. On Android, the status is kept on your phone and in your FamCents backups, so restore a backup on a new phone to keep it.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are requested from them: on iPhone at reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android through Google Play’s order history.

Your data

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Usually you don’t have to do anything. FamCents keeps its data on your phone, and it is part of your phone’s own backup: on iPhone in iCloud Backup or when you move to a new iPhone with Quick Start; on Android in Google backup, if it is turned on. Set up the new phone from that backup and FamCents opens with everything in place.

Moving between iPhone and Android, or not using phone backups? On the old phone, go to Settings → Backup → “Create backup” and save the file (for example to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email). On the new phone, use Settings → Backup → “Restore from backup”.

Can you recover my data if I lose my phone?

No. FamCents has no server, so we never have a copy of your data. Make a backup regularly, or sync with your partner: their phone then has a copy of your shared data too.

How do I delete all my data?

Settings → Data → “Erase all data”, or uninstall the app. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Voice and app lock

Voice entry doesn’t understand me

Say the amount first, then the category, then a note, for example “45 groceries supermarket”. Check that FamCents may use the microphone and speech recognition in your phone’s Settings.

I can’t unlock the app

The app lock uses your phone’s Face ID, fingerprint or passcode. If biometrics fail, use your phone’s passcode. The lock can be turned off in Settings → Security.

Something isn’t working. How do I report it?

In FamCents, go to Settings → Contact support. You can choose to include diagnostic info: the app version, your phone model and what the app did recently (screens, syncs, errors), never your amounts, notes or names. You see it in the email before you send it, and it helps us find the problem much faster.
