Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

Who we are

FamCents is developed and published by Sergiu Martin, Romania. For any privacy question or request, contact us at smartin.devsupport@gmail.com.

In short

We don’t run a server for FamCents and we don’t collect your personal data: everything you enter stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and FamCents has no analytics of its own.

Data you enter

Your transactions, categories, budgets, notes and settings are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. We do not run a server for this app and cannot see, access or sell your data.

Backups and exports

Backup files, CSV exports and Excel reports are created only when you tap the button, and you choose where to save or send them (for example iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email). Once saved there, the privacy policy of that service applies. Your phone’s own backups (iCloud or Google) may include the app’s data, depending on your phone settings.

Voice entry

When you add a transaction by voice, your phone’s own speech recognition (Apple or Google) turns your words into text. Where the phone supports it, this happens entirely on the device; otherwise the audio is processed by Apple or Google under their privacy policies. FamCents only uses the microphone while you are using voice entry and does not keep any recordings.

Sharing with your partner

When you use “Share with partner”, the app creates a file with your FamCents data and you choose how to send it (for example AirDrop, a messaging app or email). It goes only to the person you send it to; there is no FamCents server in between. The service you use to send it may handle it under its own privacy policy. With “Sync both ways automatically”, the two iPhones also connect directly to each other (Wi-Fi / Bluetooth) to send the data back; this needs the Local Network permission.

Face ID, fingerprint and passcode

The app lock uses your phone’s built-in authentication. Your face, fingerprint or passcode is checked by the phone itself; the app only learns whether unlocking succeeded.

Notifications

Reminders and budget alerts are created on your phone. No notification server is used and no data is sent.

Purchases

FamCents Pro is bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. They process the payment; we never receive your payment details. The app only asks the store whether Pro has been purchased on your account.

How your data is protected

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read, and is protected by your phone’s own security, such as device encryption and your screen lock. You can also lock the app with Face ID, fingerprint or your passcode. Backup, export and sync files are not encrypted by FamCents: send them only to people you trust and keep them somewhere safe.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep it there; we never have a copy. You can delete everything at any time with Settings → Erase all data, or by uninstalling the app. To make syncing and “Undo last sync” work, the app also keeps a short list of deleted entries and its last 3 sync snapshots on your phone; Erase all data removes these too. Copies you saved or sent yourself (backups, exports, sync files) and your phone’s own backups are not affected: delete them where you stored them. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing we could access, correct or delete on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to smartin.devsupport@gmail.com.
