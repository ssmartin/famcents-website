Private by design

Your financial data stays on your devices. No account. No cloud storage. No subscription.

  • No account, no FamCents serverYour data is stored only on your phone. We can’t see it, and there’s nothing to hack on our side.
  • You choose when to shareSyncing with your partner goes from phone to phone, only when you decide.
  • No ads, no trackingFamCents has no analytics of its own and never sells or shares your data.
  • On-device smartsReceipts are read on your phone; voice entry uses your phone’s own speech recognition.
  • Lock itProtect the app with Face ID, fingerprint or your passcode, free.
  • Your data, your copyBack up to a file, restore on any phone, export to CSV, or erase everything.
