One household. Two phones. One clear picture.

You add the groceries. Your partner adds the bills. OurCents brings your expenses together, so you both see the household’s spending, without sharing passwords or keeping a joint online account.

  1. Pair once, in about a minute. Both of you tap “Set up pairing”. One phone shows a 6-digit code; on the other, tap “Enter my partner’s code instead” and type it. With OurCents open on both phones on the same Wi-Fi, they pair and sync right away.
  2. From then on: one tap, a few seconds. With OurCents open on both phones on the same Wi-Fi, tap Sync and both are up to date – iPhone or Android, with nothing to send or accept. Two iPhones close together don’t even need Wi-Fi.
  3. Nothing gets lost. New entries, changes and deletions carry over both ways. Something looks wrong? Undo the last sync.

No OurCents account or server is involved: the code is never sent, and everything the two phones exchange is encrypted, so only they can read it. Not together? You can send a sync file through a messaging app instead; it passes through that service, under its own privacy policy.

Private by design

Your financial data stays on your devices. No sign-up. No ads. No subscription.

  • No sign-up, no OurCents serverYour data is stored on your phone. We don’t operate a server that stores your financial records.
  • You choose when to shareSyncing only starts when one of you taps Sync: directly between your phones on the same Wi-Fi (or between nearby iPhones), or with a file sent by AirDrop or a messaging app.
  • No ads, no trackingOurCents has no analytics of its own and never sells or shares your data.
  • No data collectedOurCents collects nothing about you. Voice entry uses your phone’s own speech recognition.
  • Lock itProtect the app with Face ID, fingerprint or your passcode, free.
  • Your data, your copyBack up to a file, restore on any phone, export to CSV, or erase everything.
