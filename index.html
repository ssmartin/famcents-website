One household. Two phones. One clear picture.

You add the groceries. Your partner adds the bills. FamCents brings your expenses together, so you both see the household’s spending, without sharing passwords or keeping a joint online account.

  1. Set it up once, in about a minute. Tap Sync and send the file with AirDrop, Quick Share or a messaging app. Your partner opens it and sends theirs back the same way, with one tap on “Send mine to …”. That’s it: both phones have everything.
  2. From then on: one tap, a few seconds. With FamCents open on both phones on the same Wi-Fi, tap Sync and both are up to date – iPhone or Android, with nothing to send or accept. Two iPhones close together don’t even need Wi-Fi.
  3. Nothing gets lost. New entries, changes and deletions carry over both ways. Something looks wrong? Undo the last sync.

No FamCents account or server is involved. Files sent through a messaging app pass through that service, under its own privacy policy. Direct syncs go straight between your phones.

Private by design

Your financial data stays on your devices. No account. No ads. No subscription.

  • No account, no FamCents serverYour data is stored on your phone. We don’t operate a server that stores your financial records.
  • You choose when to shareSyncing only starts when one of you taps Sync: directly between your phones on the same Wi-Fi (or between nearby iPhones), or with a file sent by AirDrop or a messaging app.
  • No ads, no trackingFamCents has no analytics of its own and never sells or shares your data.
  • No data collectedFamCents collects nothing about you. Voice entry uses your phone’s own speech recognition.
  • Lock itProtect the app with Face ID, fingerprint or your passcode, free.
  • Your data, your copyBack up to a file, restore on any phone, export to CSV, or erase everything.
