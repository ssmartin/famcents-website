What’s coming

Updated October 2026

FamCents is made by one person and keeps growing. These are the plans right now. They can change, and there are no dates – but this is what we’re working on. Everything stays private: on your phones, no account, no ads.

Working on it

Sync iPhone and Android directly

One tap to sync when both phones are on the same Wi-Fi – also between an iPhone and an Android phone, with no account and no cloud. Today this works between two Android phones (beta); an iPhone and an Android phone already sync by sending a file.

Import from your bank (Pro)

Import the transactions from your bank’s statement (CSV file): FamCents suggests a category for each, you check them, and they’re added in one go. Revolut first, more banks to follow. Everything happens on your phone.

Budgets per week, month or year

Choose a period for each category budget: a weekly budget for groceries, a monthly one for restaurants, a yearly one for holidays or gifts. Each shows what’s left in its current period.

One Pro for both partners on Android

Missing something?

Tell us what would help you most: in FamCents, go to Settings → Contact support, or email smartin.appsupport@gmail.com. Many of the changes so far came from people using the app.
